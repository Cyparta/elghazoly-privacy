# El-Gazoly-app

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

El-Gazoly-app – Appointments Made Easy ("El-Gazoly-app", "we", "our", or "us") is a digital platform for laboratory services that allows users to access and manage laboratory-related services, including viewing laboratory test results, requesting home sample collection, contacting the laboratory regarding services and prices, finding laboratory branches, providing feedback, and viewing available offers and promotions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the El-Gazoly-app application and related services.

El-Gazoly-app is a laboratory services platform. The application does not provide medical diagnosis, medical advice, treatment recommendations, or emergency medical services.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and manage our laboratory services.

Account Information

When you create an account or use El-Gazoly-app, we may collect information necessary to create, maintain, and authenticate your account.

Depending on the features you use, this information may include:

Name
Phone number
Email address
Account credentials or authentication information
Profile picture, if you choose to add one
Information necessary to identify and manage your account
Laboratory Service Information

When you use laboratory services through the application, we may process information necessary to provide and manage those services, including:

Laboratory tests or services selected by you
Information related to your laboratory service request
Sample collection information
Branch or location selected by you
Home sample collection requests
Request status and related service information
Information necessary to identify and retrieve your laboratory results
Laboratory Test Results

If you use the "My Test Results" feature, the application may allow you to access and view laboratory test results associated with your account or laboratory request.

Laboratory test results may contain health-related information. We process this information only as necessary to provide the laboratory results service, authenticate your access, maintain the security of your account, and operate the application.

Access to laboratory results may require a laboratory-provided verification code or other authentication information.

We do not use laboratory test results to provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical advice through the application.

Home Sample Collection

If you request a home sample collection service, we may collect information necessary to arrange and complete the visit, including:

Name and contact information
Home visit address or location
Requested laboratory service
Preferred date or time, where applicable
Information necessary for the laboratory team to complete the sample collection

Location or address information provided for a home visit is used to facilitate the requested service.

Communications and Inquiries

If you contact the laboratory through the application, including for inquiries about laboratory services or prices, we may process the information you provide to respond to your request.

Where the application provides communication through services such as WhatsApp or similar third-party communication platforms, your use of those services may also be subject to the privacy policies of the relevant third-party provider.

Feedback

If you use the "Your Opinion Matters" or feedback feature, we may collect and process the feedback, rating, comments, and other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information to evaluate and improve our services and user experience.

Branch Information

The application may display information about laboratory branches, including branch names, addresses, contact information, and other relevant information.

We may process information related to your selected or preferred branch when necessary to provide the requested service.

Offers and Promotions

The application may display available laboratory offers, discounts, and promotions.

We may process information necessary to display and manage these offers and to determine their availability or eligibility where applicable.

Create and manage your account

Arrange home sample collection visits
Provide access to laboratory test results
Verify access to laboratory results

We do not use information collected through the application to provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical advice.

Sharing of Information

We may share information when necessary to provide the services you request.

This may include sharing relevant information with:

The laboratory and its authorized personnel
Laboratory staff responsible for processing your tests or home sample collection requests
Trusted service providers that support the operation of the application
Hosting, database, authentication, storage, notification, analytics, and other technical service providers, where applicable
Third parties when disclosure is required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own medical advertising purposes.

Medical and Health-Related Information

Some services provided through El-Gazoly-app, particularly the laboratory test results feature, may involve health-related information.

We process such information only when necessary to provide the requested laboratory service, including allowing authorized users to access their laboratory results.

We do not use laboratory results or other health-related information to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice through the application.

Any medical interpretation, diagnosis, or treatment based on laboratory results should be obtained directly from a qualified healthcare professional.

Camera and Photo Library

El-Gazoly-app may request access to your device camera or photo library when you choose to add or change your profile picture.

Camera and photo library access is optional and is used to allow you to select or take a profile picture for your account.

We do not use your camera or photo library to collect laboratory reports, X-rays, prescriptions, or other medical documents unless a specific feature is introduced that clearly requires such access.

You can manage or disable camera and photo library permissions at any time through your device settings.

Photos selected as profile pictures may be uploaded to our servers or service providers as necessary to store and display your profile picture within the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
